## Introduction

In December 2010, a brief video recorded by former NBA star Manu Ginóbili showing unusual lights over Santa Monica quickly became another celebrity-linked UFO story. What makes the incident notable is not that it remained mysterious, but that it became a well-documented example of a widely shared sighting receiving a plausible, evidence-based explanation. Subsequent investigation linked the lights to night skydivers from the Red Bull Air Force performing with pyrotechnic flares, illustrating how public attention can amplify not only extraordinary claims but also well-supported corrections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fiba.basketball/en/news/arg-ginobili-red-bull-gives-you-a-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fiba.basketball">[FIBA Basketball]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fiba.basketball</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ARG – Ginobili: Red Bull gives you… a UFO sightingDecember 15, 2010 — “I was watching the news and I see them reporting on two UFO sighti...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 15, 2010</span></span></span>

## The Santa Monica lights report

The incident occurred while Ginóbili was in Santa Monica and noticed bright lights moving in ways that appeared unusual against the evening sky. He recorded the object and shared the footage, and the video spread rapidly through sports and entertainment media, often being framed as a possible UFO sighting rather than simply an unidentified aerial object.

The footage itself was intriguing because the lights appeared to change direction and did not immediately resemble a conventional aircraft. Without additional context, viewers were left to interpret what they were seeing, and the involvement of a well-known athlete ensured that the story reached audiences far beyond the usual UFO community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://contrailscience.com/manu-ginobilis-santa-monica-silver-surfer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: contrailscience.com">[contrailscience.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">contrailscience.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">manu ginobilis santa monica silver surfer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">manu-ginobili-ufo-video-san-antonio-spurs/. ## Red Bull Air Force and the flare explanation

The strongest explanation emerged when members of the Red Bull Air Force recognised the television coverage. Skydiver Jon DeVore stated that he immediately recognised the reported "UFO" because he and fellow jumpers had been conducting night parachute demonstrations using illuminated flares on the same evenings the sightings were reported.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fiba.basketball/en/news/arg-ginobili-red-bull-gives-you-a-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fiba.basketball">[FIBA Basketball]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fiba.basketball</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ARG – Ginobili: Red Bull gives you… a UFO sightingDecember 15, 2010 — “I was watching the news and I see them reporting on two UFO sighti...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 15, 2010</span></span></span>

Independent analysis strengthened that account. Using the filming locations visible in separate videos, investigator Mick West reconstructed the lines of sight in Google Earth and triangulated the apparent position of the lights. Rather than being a distant craft high in the atmosphere, the reconstruction suggested the object was relatively close to the shoreline, roughly over the beach and at an altitude consistent with skydiving operations. The apparent changes in speed and direction also fit nearby parachutists carrying bright flares far better than a distant aircraft or unknown object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://contrailscience.com/manu-ginobilis-santa-monica-silver-surfer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: contrailscience.com">[contrailscience.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">contrailscience.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">manu ginobilis santa monica silver surfer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">manu-ginobili-ufo-video-san-antonio-spurs/. ## How publicity helped spread the correction

Celebrity involvement initially increased public interest in the mystery. Sports media, entertainment outlets and UFO websites all circulated the video, giving the impression that a prominent athlete had witnessed something extraordinary.

However, the same visibility also made correction easier. Because the footage reached a large audience, people connected with the Red Bull Air Force saw the reports and publicly identified the likely source of the lights. The incident therefore became unusual among celebrity UFO stories: widespread publicity did not simply reinforce speculation but helped connect the event with people who had direct knowledge of the activity responsible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fiba.basketball/en/news/arg-ginobili-red-bull-gives-you-a-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fiba.basketball">[FIBA Basketball]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fiba.basketball</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ARG – Ginobili: Red Bull gives you… a UFO sightingDecember 15, 2010 — “I was watching the news and I see them reporting on two UFO sighti...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 15, 2010</span></span></span>

The case also demonstrates the value of combining witness testimony with independent reconstruction. Rather than arguing over belief, investigators compared camera locations, viewing geometry and known events occurring in the area. ## Why the case remains instructive

Within discussions of celebrities and UFOs, the Ginóbili incident stands out because it illustrates the distinction between an unexplained observation and evidence for an extraordinary phenomenon. Ginóbili accurately reported seeing something he could not identify. That fact alone did not establish what the object was.

The subsequent identification of Red Bull Air Force skydivers using flares shows how unusual aerial displays can produce convincing UFO reports under ordinary viewing conditions. It also demonstrates that a celebrity's status neither validates nor invalidates a sighting; the decisive factor is the quality of the follow-up evidence.
