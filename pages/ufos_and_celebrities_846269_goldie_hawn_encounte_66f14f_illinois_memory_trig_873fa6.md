## Introduction

A distinctive feature of Goldie Hawn’s UFO narrative is that one of its most emotionally significant details did not appear in her original recollection. Instead, Hawn has said that many years after the alleged encounter, a conversation with an astrophysicist at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign prompted a vivid new memory: the sensation that the [beings]({{ 'beings/' | relative_url }}) had touched her face. This episode matters because it shifts the story from being only an account of an unusual experience to a case about how memory itself can change over time. ## Did Talking About It Change the Memory?

### The reported astrophysicist conversation

According to Hawn's account, the turning point came during a visit to the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. She has said that she met an astrophysicist who told her he had spent roughly 25 years researching reports of alien encounters and wanted to hear her own experience. Hawn has not publicly identified the researcher, and there is no publicly available academic record documenting the interview itself. The event is therefore known only through her later retellings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2023/10/goldie-hawn-knows-that-aliens-have-touched-her-face?srsltid=AfmBOormHstYUFNwx4uCrgTpwZzlrhUpyAjmW0aNl6ExyXVWMtX2jiTQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vanityfair.com">[Vanity Fair]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vanityfair.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Vanity Fair Goldie Hawn Knows That Aliens Have Touched Her Face</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vanity FairGoldie Hawn Knows That Aliens Have Touched Her FaceOctober 23, 2023 — 23 Oct 2023 — Hawn tells us that years later, she discus...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2023</span></span></span>

### Recovered detail and emotional force

The newly recalled element was not another visual description of the alleged beings but a tactile sensation. Hawn has repeatedly said that, during the conversation, she suddenly remembered them touching her face.

She has described the sensation in intensely spiritual language, saying it "felt like the finger of God" and characterising it as overwhelmingly benevolent, loving and filled with light. ## Why memory triggers complicate evidence

From a historical perspective, the University of Illinois conversation is significant precisely because it demonstrates the difference between an experience and a later recollection of that experience.

Research in cognitive psychology shows that autobiographical memories are reconstructive rather than fixed recordings. Recalling an event can strengthen some elements, weaken others and occasionally introduce new associations. Emotional interviews, repeated storytelling and exposure to questions that encourage reflection can all alter how a memory is organised and experienced, even when the person sincerely believes the recollection is accurate. This does not mean the memory is fabricated, but neither does it establish that every newly remembered detail corresponds to an external event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2023-10-27/goldie-hawn-alien-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latimes.com">[Los Angeles Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">goldie hawn alien encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Los Angeles TimesGoldie Hawn recounts her paralyzing alien encounter27 Oct 2023 — Goldie Hawn recounted an alien encounter that left her...</span></span></span>

Hawn herself appears to present the recovered memory as genuine rather than speculative. However, because the additional detail surfaced decades after the alleged encounter, historians and psychologists would generally distinguish between:

## Why this moment matters within Hawn's narrative

The University of Illinois discussion occupies a unique place in Hawn's account because it marks the point at which an uncertain youthful experience became a coherent personal narrative.

Without that conversation, her story would largely consist of an unexplained episode involving paralysis, unusual perceptions and lingering uncertainty. Afterward, it acquired a defining emotional centre: the remembered touch that she interpreted as loving rather than threatening. That shift explains why later retellings consistently devote considerable attention to the interview itself.
