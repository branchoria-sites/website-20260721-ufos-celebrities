## Introduction

Goldie Hawn's description of the beings she says she encountered is one of the most enduring parts of her UFO story because it combines unusually specific visual details with repeated acknowledgements that her perception was uncertain. Rather than presenting a detailed anatomical description of extraterrestrials, Hawn consistently recalls a small group of silver figures with triangular-shaped heads seen through the window of a parked car while she was in a state she also describes as involving sudden sleepiness, paralysis and confusion about whether the experience was "real or not real". Those characteristics make the imagery noteworthy less as physical evidence than as an example of how vivid visual memories can persist, evolve and acquire personal meaning over decades.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2023-10-27/goldie-hawn-alien-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latimes.com">[Los Angeles Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I think I made contact with outer space.</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Los Angeles TimesGoldie Hawn recounts her paralyzing alien encounterOctober 28, 2023 — 27 Oct 2023 — Goldie Hawn recounted an alien encou...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2023</span></span></span>

## The silver figures at the window

Across Hawn's public retellings, the visual appearance of the beings has remained strikingly consistent. She has repeatedly described seeing "two or three" small figures outside the car window whose heads appeared triangular. They were silver in colour, had a slash-like mouth, a tiny nose and no visible ears. Rather than behaving dramatically, she remembered them pointing towards her while seeming to communicate among themselves, accompanied by what she described as a droning sound.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2023-10-27/goldie-hawn-alien-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latimes.com">[Los Angeles Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I think I made contact with outer space.</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Los Angeles TimesGoldie Hawn recounts her paralyzing alien encounterOctober 28, 2023 — 27 Oct 2023 — Goldie Hawn recounted an alien encou...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2023</span></span></span>

What makes these details memorable is their selectivity. Hawn does not claim to remember a complete body, clothing or surrounding craft with comparable clarity. Instead, the image centres almost entirely on faces and heads. This concentration on a few distinctive visual features is common in autobiographical memories of emotionally intense events, where certain elements become highly stable while other contextual details remain fragmentary.

The triangular head itself has become the defining image because it differs from the more familiar "large grey alien" popularised in later popular culture. Whether that difference reflects an original perception, later interpretation or the natural simplification of memory cannot be determined from Hawn's account alone. The available evidence is simply that this feature has remained remarkably consistent in her own retelling over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2023-10-27/goldie-hawn-alien-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latimes.com">[Los Angeles Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I think I made contact with outer space.</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Los Angeles TimesGoldie Hawn recounts her paralyzing alien encounterOctober 28, 2023 — 27 Oct 2023 — Goldie Hawn recounted an alien encou...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2023</span></span></span>

## Specific details and uncertain perception

The remembered appearance of the beings cannot easily be separated from the state in which Hawn says she experienced them. She recalls becoming overwhelmingly sleepy while resting in a friend's car, hearing a high-frequency sound, finding herself unable to move and only then seeing the silver figures outside the window. She has also repeatedly said she did not know whether the experience was "real or not real" while it was happening.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2023-10-27/goldie-hawn-alien-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latimes.com">[Los Angeles Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I think I made contact with outer space.</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Los Angeles TimesGoldie Hawn recounts her paralyzing alien encounterOctober 28, 2023 — 27 Oct 2023 — Goldie Hawn recounted an alien encou...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2023</span></span></span>

This combination matters because it changes how the imagery is interpreted. The description functions simultaneously as:

* an experience remembered from the boundary between sleeping and waking,

Unlike many UFO narratives that emphasise certainty, Hawn leaves ambiguity in place. The visual precision of the triangular heads exists alongside uncertainty about the overall reality of the encounter itself.

That ambiguity has attracted discussion because experiences involving temporary paralysis and vivid sensory impressions are also reported in accounts of [sleep paralysis]({{ 'sleep-paralysis/' | relative_url }}) and related transitional sleep states. ## How repeated retelling shapes the imagery

The image of the triangular-headed beings has become more emotionally significant through repetition rather than through the addition of many new visual details.

When Hawn first publicly described the encounter in detail through *Time to Walk*, the emphasis was on the beings' appearance and the paralysis she experienced. Later interviews, including a 2026 appearance on *Jimmy Kimmel Live*, largely preserved the same visual description while expanding on the emotional impact of remembering the encounter, particularly the later recollection that one of the beings touched her face with what she described as extraordinary gentleness. The emotional memory developed more than the visual one.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://people.com/goldie-hawn-recounts-meeting-2-aliens-with-triangular-shaped-heads-11968111" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: people.com">[People.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">people.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">government, describing a deeply emotional moment when she realized the aliens had touched her face, a sensation she likened to &quot;the finge...</span></span></span>

This pattern is notable because autobiographical memories often become organised around a stable visual image that serves as an anchor for later reflection. In Hawn's case, the silver triangular heads remain that anchor. Subsequent retellings have not substantially altered their appearance but have placed increasing emphasis on what the encounter meant to her personally.

The consistency of the imagery therefore should not be confused with independent corroboration. The repeated description demonstrates stability within Hawn's own memory narrative rather than external verification of the event. For readers interested in celebrity UFO accounts, the triangular-headed beings matter chiefly because they illustrate how a few vivid visual features can become the enduring symbolic centre of a remembered extraordinary experience, even while the witness continues to acknowledge uncertainty about what actually occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2023-10-27/goldie-hawn-alien-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: latimes.com">[Los Angeles Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">latimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I think I made contact with outer space.</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Los Angeles TimesGoldie Hawn recounts her paralyzing alien encounterOctober 28, 2023 — 27 Oct 2023 — Goldie Hawn recounted an alien encou...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2023</span></span></span>
