## Introduction

As official discussion increasingly shifted from the language of UFOs to unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP), celebrity engagement did not disappear. Instead, it continued largely through entertainment, personal storytelling and paranormal media. Kesha and Demi Lovato illustrate this transition particularly well. Rather than presenting themselves as investigators producing scientific evidence, both artists openly discussed their own beliefs and experiences, then incorporated those interests into [music]({{ 'music/' | relative_url }}), podcasts and television. Their work shows how celebrity UFO culture evolved alongside—not in direct competition with—the more formal policy and defence conversations that emerged after 2017.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://variety.com/2021/tv/news/demi-lovato-ufo-series-peacock-1234970606/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: variety.com">[Variety]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">variety.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">demi lovato ufo series peacock 1234970606</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Demi Lovato to Host Unscripted UFO Investigation Series...11 May 2021 — Demi Lovato will find out if the truth is out there in a...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2021</span></span></span>

## Kesha's Joshua Tree inspiration

Kesha has repeatedly said that an experience in California's Joshua Tree inspired the imagery behind her 2017 album *Rainbow*. In interviews promoting the album, she described seeing several unexplained lights or "spaceships" in the night sky while sitting on rocks in the desert. She acknowledged that she did not photograph the event, presenting it as a deeply personal experience rather than documented evidence. The encounter became part of the album's creative mythology rather than a claim intended for scientific verification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.teenvogue.com/story/kesha-new-album-rainbow-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: teenvogue.com">[Teen Vogue]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">teenvogue.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Teen Vogue A UFO Sighting Inspired Kesha&#x27;s New Album Rainbow</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">On the Zach Sang Show, she recalled seeing multiple spaceships in the sky, which sparked the theme for the album, evident in the song tit...</span></span></span>

The experience influenced the record's visual language as well as its themes. *Rainbow* includes the song "Spaceship", while UFO imagery also appears in the album artwork. Although the album primarily addresses recovery, resilience and personal freedom following years of legal and professional turmoil, Kesha used extraterrestrial symbolism as another expression of feeling different, searching for belonging and imagining possibilities beyond ordinary experience.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.teenvogue.com/story/kesha-new-album-rainbow-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: teenvogue.com">[Teen Vogue]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">teenvogue.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Teen Vogue A UFO Sighting Inspired Kesha&#x27;s New Album Rainbow</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">On the Zach Sang Show, she recalled seeing multiple spaceships in the sky, which sparked the theme for the album, evident in the song tit...</span></span></span>

Her interest in paranormal subjects extended beyond music. In 2020 she launched the podcast *Kesha and the Creepies*, featuring discussions about ghosts, cryptids, UFOs and alternative spirituality with musicians, paranormal researchers and entertainers. UFO belief was treated as one element within a broader fascination with unusual experiences rather than as a political issue or campaign for government disclosure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kesha" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

That trajectory continued with the Discovery+ television series *Conjuring Kesha* in 2022. Although the programme explored a range of paranormal topics rather than UFOs alone, it reinforced Kesha's public identity as a celebrity interested in supernatural mysteries, demonstrating how paranormal entertainment remained commercially viable during the same period that UAP discussions were becoming more institutionalised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjuring_Kesha" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Conjuring Kesha</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Conjuring Kesha</span></span></span>

## Demi Lovato's UFO television role

Demi Lovato's engagement with UFOs became even more explicit by making the subject itself the focus of a television series. Peacock's four-part *Unidentified with Demi Lovato*, released in 2021, followed Lovato, sister Dallas Lovato and friend Matthew Scott Montgomery as they visited reported UFO locations, interviewed experiencers and spoke with researchers and witnesses. The programme blended travel documentary, reality television and paranormal investigation rather than presenting itself as a scientific inquiry.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://variety.com/2021/tv/news/demi-lovato-ufo-series-peacock-1234970606/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: variety.com">[Variety]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">variety.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">demi lovato ufo series peacock 1234970606</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Demi Lovato to Host Unscripted UFO Investigation Series...11 May 2021 — Demi Lovato will find out if the truth is out there in a...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2021</span></span></span>

Lovato explained that their fascination grew from what they considered a personal experience in Joshua Tree, where they believed they had encountered an unexplained phenomenon. Throughout the series they described themselves as a "UFO experiencer" and explored ideas about extraterrestrial contact, while also acknowledging that many people dismiss such claims. The programme mixed sincere personal belief with humour and entertainment, making viewers participants in Lovato's search rather than promising definitive answers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.udiscovermusic.com/news/demi-lovato-unidentified-with-demi-lovato/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: udiscovermusic.com">[uDiscover Music]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">udiscovermusic.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">demi lovato unidentified with demi lovato</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">uDiscover MusicDemi Lovato Investigates UFOs In &#x27;Unidentified With...13 Sept 2021 — “I had this crazy experience that happened to me in...</span></span></span>

The series also introduced audiences to parts of modern UFO culture beyond headline news, including experiencer communities, paranormal investigators and discussions of alleged contact experiences. Critics noted that the show often emphasised Lovato's personal journey and emotional openness as much as the possibility of extraterrestrial life, placing self-exploration alongside paranormal curiosity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2021/10/all-the-questions-i-had-while-watching-unidentified-with-demi-lovato" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vanityfair.com">[Vanity Fair]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vanityfair.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Vanity Fair All The Questions I Had While Watching Unidentified with Demi Lovato</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Accompanied by their friend and sister, Demi travels California meeting UFO enthusiasts and therapists specializing in extraterrestrial e...</span></span></span>

An important feature of *Unidentified* was its willingness to present belief as a lived identity rather than as a claim that government investigations had already validated. Even as official agencies were discussing unexplained aerial observations using the newer UAP terminology, Lovato's programme remained rooted in the older popular culture traditions of UFO hunting, witness testimony and paranormal exploration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://variety.com/2021/tv/news/demi-lovato-ufo-series-peacock-1234970606/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: variety.com">[Variety]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">variety.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">demi lovato ufo series peacock 1234970606</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Demi Lovato to Host Unscripted UFO Investigation Series...11 May 2021 — Demi Lovato will find out if the truth is out there in a...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2021</span></span></span>

## Why celebrity belief survived the UAP era

Kesha and Demi Lovato demonstrate that celebrity UFO culture did not simply evolve into [advocacy]({{ 'advocacy/' | relative_url }}) for official UAP investigations. Instead, it split into a parallel entertainment tradition with its own audiences, formats and goals.
